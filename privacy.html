Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Reign of Rivals (the “game”) collects, how it is used, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the team that makes and runs Reign of Rivals. You can contact us at reignofrivals@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Android app, the game servers it connects to, and the account deletion page on our website.

1. Summary

  • The game has no ads and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • The game may offer optional in-app purchases of Crowns, its virtual currency. Payments are processed by the app store (Google Play on Android), and we do not receive your full payment card details (section 2.6).
  • We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • When you tap Continue on the first screen, our server creates a guest account for you. It is identified by random IDs and a display name, not by an email or password.
  • Where the game offers it, you can optionally link a Google account on Android so you can bring your kingdom to a new device. We store only an ID for that Google account, not your email address (section 2.1).
  • We store your game progress, your display name, and the chat messages you send. Chat messages are kept with no fixed end date, including after you delete your account (shown as “Deleted Player”).
  • Push notifications are optional. If you allow them, you choose which kinds you get in Settings › NOTIFICATIONS (section 2.7).
  • You can delete your account in the game (Settings › ACCOUNT › ACCOUNT DELETION), on our website, or by email. See Delete your account.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and sign-in information

  • Account ID and player ID. Random identifiers our server creates for your guest account.
  • Display name. New players get a generated name such as Guest-1a2b3c4d5e. You can change it in your Profile. We keep a record of each name change (old name, new name, time) to process renames and to remove all your names if you delete your account.
  • Player flag. The emblem you design from the game's built-in patterns, colours and symbols.
  • Terms acceptance. The version of our Terms of Service you accepted and when. We need this before you can use chat and other social features.
  • Sign-in sessions. A hashed (scrambled) copy of your sign-in token, the platform name your device reports (for example “Android”), and timestamps. This is not a device identifier.
  • Push notification token (only if you allow notifications). If you allow notifications on your Android device, the game receives a push token from Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. We store it with your account and the platform name so we can send you the game notifications described in section 2.7, such as a warning when an enemy Attack is heading for one of your cities or Shrines. We use it for nothing else.
  • Restore codes. If you lose access to your device, our support team can issue a one-time restore code. We store only a hashed copy. It expires after 15 minutes and can be used once.
  • Linked Google account (optional, Android only, where the game offers it). If you tap LINK GOOGLE in Settings › ACCOUNT, Google's sign-in screen gives our server a signed sign-in token. Our server checks it with Google's public keys and keeps only the permanent ID Google uses for your Google account (Google calls it the “subject”), when you linked it and when it was last used to restore your kingdom. The token also contains your email address, name and profile picture; we do not store them. The same applies when you use RESTORE WITH GOOGLE on a new device, or Google sign-in on our account deletion page. You can unlink at any time; unlinking or deleting your account deletes the stored ID. To stop a sign-in being reused, we keep a scrambled one-time code from each sign-in for about a day.

2.2 Game data

Everything that makes up your kingdom and its history, for example: your cities, troops, Gold, Crowns, Gear, Runes, chests and other items; your Level, stats, quests and rewards; Marches, battles and Scout reports; Season, League and ranking results; Alliance membership and role; friends and friend requests; your mute and block lists; and when you were last active. It also includes:

  • Daily login rewards. A record of each daily reward you claim: the date, which day of the reward track it was, what it gave you, and a code that stops the same claim being paid twice.
  • Season recaps. When a Season ends, our server saves a short personal summary of your Season, for example how many cities you captured and held, battles won and lost, your final rank, Glory and League result, your Alliance's placing, Shrines held and captured, your biggest battle (the other side is recorded only as a kind, such as “Player” or “Pirate”, never by name), and on how many days you played. To count those days, the server notes once a day that you were active in the Season. Only you can see your recaps.

2.3 Messages and other things you write

  • Chat. Messages you send in Global chat (visible to players in your World), Alliance chat (visible to your Alliance's members), and private messages (only between two players who are friends). Messages are plain text. Some offensive words are automatically replaced with asterisks when you post; the masked version is what we store.
  • Alliance text. Alliance names, tags and announcements you write as a leader or officer.
  • Alliance Marks. If you mark a location on the map for your Alliance (ATTACK TARGET, DEFEND or REGROUP POINT), we store the location, the kind of mark, any note you add (up to 60 characters), your player ID and name, and when it was placed, cleared or expires. Notes are filtered like chat. If you choose POST TO ALLIANCE CHAT, the mark is also posted as your own Alliance chat message.
  • Reports about other players. If you report a message, a player's profile or an Alliance's public text, we store the reason you chose, any note you add, a snapshot of the reported message and the players' names at that moment, up to five nearby messages from the same chat (only a chat you could already read), your World and Season, and the game version and platform name, so a moderator can review it. The reported player is never told who reported them.
  • Bug and cheating reports. If you send a report from Settings › SUPPORT, we store your description, the category you chose, any player name you mention, the game version, platform name, device model and operating-system version shown on the form, and which screen you sent it from. Please don't include passwords, restore codes or payment details; the server removes text that looks like a sign-in token.
  • Chat settings. Your chat language, whether Auto-Translate is on, and your profanity-filter setting.

2.4 Technical and diagnostic information

  • Gameplay analytics (our own, no third-party SDK). When the game talks to our server, we record your player ID, which game action was requested (for example “send a March”), whether it succeeded, an error code if it failed, the time, and your game version. The game also tells our server when it is opened, resumed, and every five minutes while it is open, so we can count active players and sessions. These records do not contain the content of your requests, chat text, passwords, restore codes, IP addresses or device identifiers. They are deleted after 90 days.
  • New-player milestones (our own analytics). For each player we record when certain first steps happened: when your starter area was created, your first city capture, the first chest you opened and the first time you joined an Alliance. Together with the times you completed the Beginner quests, we use them only to make reports for our team that count, per sign-up day, how many new players reached each step and how long it took. These reports show totals only, never individual players: any number from 1 to 4, and any sign-up day with fewer than 5 players, is hidden.
  • IP address. Like any internet service, our server sees your device's IP address when it connects. The game server uses it in memory to limit repeated sign-in attempts and does not save it in the game database. Our server's system and web-server logs may record IP addresses, times and requested addresses for security and troubleshooting; these logs are kept for a limited time and are overwritten as they rotate. We do not use your IP address to work out your location.
  • Game engine diagnostics. The game is built with the Unity engine (Unity Technologies). From version 1.7.1 the game has Unity's engine diagnostics turned off. Earlier test versions had them on; in those versions Unity may collect technical diagnostics such as crash and performance information, device model, operating-system version and an app-install identifier, under Unity's own privacy policy.

2.5 Information stored on your device

The game stores your sign-in credential in your device's protected storage (Android Keystore), and keeps game settings such as sound volume, display and vibration settings, the language you chose for the game's menus, chat settings and any restore code you typed. Logging out removes the sign-in credential. Deleting your account in the game removes the sign-in credential and any saved codes. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game removes all of it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your sign-in; to get back into the same kingdom you then need a restore code from support or, where the game offers Google linking, a linked Google account (RESTORE WITH GOOGLE).

2.6 Purchases

Reign of Rivals may offer optional in-app purchases of Crowns, the game's virtual currency. Purchases are made through the app store you installed the game from (on Android, Google Play), and the store processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

When you buy something, we process limited information about the purchase and link it to your account, so that we can check the purchase is genuine, deliver your Crowns, prevent fraud, handle refunds, reversals and support requests, and keep the records we need for accounting and legal reasons.

The purchase information we keep is: which Crown pack you bought and how many Crowns it gave you, the Google Play order number, a coded reference to the purchase, when you bought it, and whether it is completed, pending, cancelled or refunded (and, for a refund, whether we have reviewed it). To check a purchase, the game sends our server the purchase reference that Google Play gives it, and our server confirms the purchase directly with Google. We keep that reference itself only until the purchase is fully processed. When you start a purchase, the game also gives Google Play a coded identifier for your game account so the purchase reaches the right account; it does not tell Google who you are in the game.

2.7 Notification settings

If you allow notifications on your device, the game can send you these push notifications. You can turn each kind on or off in Settings › NOTIFICATIONS, and the switches are saved with your account so they also apply when the game is closed:

  • Incoming Attack: an enemy Attack is heading for one of your cities or Shrines, and when it arrives.
  • City lost: one of your cities or Shrines was captured, and by whom (as in your battle report).
  • Pirates landed: pirates landed on an island where you hold a city.
  • Rally or Regroup complete: all the troops you sent in a Rally or Regroup have arrived (off unless you turn it on).
  • Alliance Mark: another member of your Alliance marked a location, with their name and note.
  • Reminders: your daily reward is ready to claim, or your Shield ends soon. Each reminder has its own switch.

We store your switches, whether quiet hours are on and their times (22:00 to 08:00 by default), and your device's time-zone offset (for example UTC+2), which the game sends so that quiet hours follow your local time. The offset is not your location, and we use it only for quiet hours and for timing reminders. During quiet hours the game holds back less urgent notifications; incoming-Attack and city-lost notifications still come through. Until your device has reported its offset, nothing is held back. We also keep short records of the notifications we send, so that each one is sent only once and not too often.

2.8 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, videos, audio, files, calendar, browsing history, health information, full payment card or bank account details, or advertising ID. The game sends push notifications only for the game events listed in section 2.7, and only if you allow notifications on your device. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game: create and sign in to your account, keep your kingdom and the shared World up to date, resolve battles, show rankings, deliver chat, Inbox messages and Alliance Marks, send the push notifications you allow (section 2.7), pay daily login rewards, show your Season recaps, translate chat when you ask, and restore your account if you lose your device (with a restore code or, where offered, a linked Google account).
  • To handle purchases: check that purchases are genuine, deliver Crowns, prevent fraud, handle refunds and support requests, and keep purchase records.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: limit repeated sign-in attempts, review reports, mask or remove abusive messages, change offensive names, and suspend accounts that break the rules.
  • To support you: answer questions and handle deletion and recovery requests.
  • To improve the game: understand, in aggregate, which features are used, where new players stop (using the new-player reports in section 2.4, which show totals only), and which errors happen.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom: we provide the game to you under our Terms of Service (performance of a contract); we keep the game secure, moderate it and measure how it is used because we have a legitimate interest in running a safe, working game; we keep records the law requires us to keep, such as purchase records, to meet our legal obligations; and where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

4. Who can see your information

Other players can see your public game identity: your display name, flag, Level, Glory (rounded), League, Alliance and role, number of cities, rankings, combat statistics and past Season results, your cities on the World map, and the chat messages you send to them or to a channel they can read. Alliance members can see roughly when you were last active (for example “Online” or “3 hours ago”), and the Alliance Marks you place, with your name and note, until each mark expires (after 6 hours) or is cleared. Other players cannot see your troop counts, Gold, Crowns, Gear, Runes, chests or other items, your daily rewards, your Season recaps, your notification settings, your private Marches or your private starter cities, unless the game rules reveal a specific detail (for example, a Scout report on one of your cities, or a battle report sent to both sides).

Muting and blocking only affect communication. Muting hides a player's Global and Alliance chat messages from you; blocking also stops private messages between you in both directions, without telling the other player. Neither changes battles, scouting, rankings, what either of you can see on the map, or Alliance membership.

Moderators on our team can read reports, the reported messages and the nearby chat lines attached to them. Team members we give access to can see the new-player reports described in section 2.4, which contain totals only and no player names or IDs.

Service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Hosting: our game server and database run on a cloud server we rent from a hosting provider.
  • Chat translation (Google Cloud Translation, by Google): players can translate chat into one of 12 languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified)). When a player taps TRANSLATE on a message, or has AUTO-TRANSLATE turned on, our server sends only the message text and the language to translate into to Google Cloud Translation. We don't send Google your player ID, your name, your IP address or any other account data, and the game app itself never contacts Google. Removed messages, Alliance system notices and messages from players you muted or blocked are never sent. Our server keeps each translation for up to 90 days so the same message isn't translated twice, and deletes it sooner if the message is removed or its author deletes their account. Google processes the text as our service provider under its Google Cloud terms. If translation is unavailable, chat keeps working and you see the original text.
  • Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging, by Google): when there is a notification for you (section 2.7), our server sends Google your device's push token and the notification itself. Depending on the kind, that is the name of your city or Shrine and when an Attack arrives; the name of the city you lost and of the player who captured it; that pirates landed; that your Rally or Regroup is complete; an Alliance member's name, Alliance tag, the marked location and their note; or a short reminder. It never includes troop counts, your Gold or items, or any other private game data. Google delivers the notification to your device as our service provider under its Firebase terms. Notifications expire if they can't be delivered in time.
  • Google sign-in (optional, by Google, where the game offers it): if you link a Google account or use Google sign-in to restore or delete your kingdom, Google shows its own sign-in screen and tells our server the result as described in section 2.1. Google then knows that you signed in to Reign of Rivals, under Google's own privacy policy. Our account deletion page loads Google's sign-in button from Google only for this purpose.
  • Game engine: Unity Technologies, for the engine diagnostics described in section 2.4 (earlier test versions only).

App store: if you make a purchase, Google Play (or the store you installed the game from) handles the payment and your payment details as an independent company under its own terms and privacy policy, and tells our server about the purchase so we can verify and deliver it.

Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect players, the game or others.

Transfer of the game: if the game moves to a new operator, your information may move with it, and this policy continues to apply until it is changed.

6. Deleting your account

In the game: open Settings (the cog button at the top right) › ACCOUNT › ACCOUNT DELETION › CONFIRM DELETE ACCOUNT. This deletes the account at once.

Without the app: use our Delete your account page. You show the kingdom is yours with a restore code from support or, where the game offers Google linking, by signing in with the Google account you linked in the game, and then confirm. The account is deleted automatically 7 days later; until then you can keep it by opening the game and tapping KEEP MY ACCOUNT in Settings › ACCOUNT, or by emailing us.

By email: write to reignofrivals@gmail.com with your exact player name. Player names are public in the game, so a name alone doesn't prove ownership. If you bought Crowns, include a Google Play order number from your receipt: that proves the account is yours and the account is deleted 7 days later. Without such proof we still accept the request, but the account is deleted only once it has gone 14 days without being used, and the request is cancelled if the account is played again in the meantime, so that nobody can delete a kingdom someone else is still playing. We never send a restore code in reply to a deletion request. Accounts that pass these checks are deleted within 30 days of the request.

When an account is deleted:

  • It can never sign in again. All sessions end and unused restore codes stop working.
  • Your display name becomes “Deleted Player” everywhere it appears, including chat history, Alliance notices, reports, rankings history, Season results and city names that were based on your name. Your former names become available to other players.
  • Your Alliance place is released, stored device labels and push notification tokens are deleted, a linked Google account ID is deleted, and your analytics activity records are deleted or unlinked from your player ID.
  • Your own mute and block lists and your chat settings (chat language, Auto-Translate, profanity filter) are deleted, translations of your messages are deleted, and the device model and operating-system version are removed from any bug or cheating reports you sent.
  • Your starter area is freed for new players if nothing else in the World depends on it: the only land you hold is your Main City and your five starter cities, and no other player's March or safety report involves it. Your own Marches to or from that area are removed with it. Land you gave up earlier with Fresh Start has already become public neutral land and stays that way.

Some information is kept after deletion. It no longer carries your name and is no longer linked to any way of signing in, so it can't be used or restored by you or anyone else:

  • For 30 days, then deleted: your Gear, Runes, chests and other items, Crowns balance, quest progress, Inbox mail, friend list, and the records of your old sign-in sessions and restore codes.
  • Game records, with no fixed end date: your account and player ID (random identifiers), League profile, your Terms acceptance record, promo codes you redeemed, your daily login reward claims, your Season recaps, your new-player milestone times and your notification settings. Other players' mute and block lists that include you are their own settings and stay.
  • Shared World records: if your starter area can't be freed (for example, because you captured other cities, or another player's March or safety report involves it), your cities stay on the map without your name until that Season's World is cleaned up (about 30 days after the Season ends); battle and Scout reports and Season results remain for the other players involved, with no fixed end date.
  • Messages: the text of chat and private messages you sent stays (shown as “Deleted Player”), with no fixed end date. Translations of your messages are deleted.
  • Safety records: reports about abuse, bugs and cheating that involve you (including the reported message and its nearby messages) are kept for 1 year after deletion, then deleted. Records of moderation actions keep your player ID with no fixed end date.
  • Purchase records: kept for up to 3 years after each purchase for refunds, fraud prevention, and accounting and legal obligations (section 5), without your name. Deleting your account does not refund purchases. A purchase that is still waiting for payment when you delete your account is not delivered, and Google Play cancels or refunds it.
  • Backups and logs: copies may remain in server backups for up to 90 days and in server logs for up to 30 days.

7. Security

The game connects to our servers over encrypted HTTPS. Sign-in tokens and restore codes are stored on our server only in hashed form, and our administrative tools require a secret key. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change your display name in your Profile. Choose your chat language and turn Auto-Translate and the profanity filter on or off in Settings. Mute or block players, and report abusive messages or names.
  • Choose which kinds of notifications you get, and turn quiet hours on or off, in Settings › NOTIFICATIONS. You can turn all notifications off at any time in your device's notification settings for the game, or by not allowing them when the game asks.
  • Delete your account at any time (section 6).
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict some uses, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. To make a request, email reignofrivals@gmail.com with your exact player name. We may ask for details that help us confirm the account is yours.

9. Children

Reign of Rivals is intended for users age 14 and older and is not directed at children. You must be at least 14 to play (see our Terms of Service). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has created an account, email reignofrivals@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. International users

Our game server is hosted in a data centre that may be outside your country, and Google Cloud Translation and Firebase Cloud Messaging (when used) may process data in other countries. By using the game, your information may be processed outside your country, with the protections described in this policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes (for example, if we change how purchases or notifications work, or add new sign-in methods such as Sign in with Apple). We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for important changes, tell players in the game.

The game's menus can be shown in several languages, but this policy is available in English only.
