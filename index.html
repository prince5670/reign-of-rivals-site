Build a kingdom. Beat your rivals.

Reign of Rivals is a persistent multiplayer strategy game. Every city you own produces Gold and troops around the clock, even while you are away. Upgrade cities instantly, send your armies out, and race other players for Glory.

Conquer

Start with a protected Main City and five private neutral cities to capture. March armies over land and sea. Battles are deterministic: scout first, check the estimate, then strike.

Fight fair

Glory measures your cities and troops. Attacking far weaker players earns less, or nothing, so the best rewards come from worthy rivals. Shields protect your cities for hours at a time.

Join an Alliance

Alliances of up to 30 players share a private chat, daily Gold Gifts and timed Alliance Quests. Capture Shrines to give your whole Alliance a bonus.

Seasons

Each Season lasts 14 days in a fresh World, and new islands open as it goes on. Your Gear, Runes, unopened chests, League and Alliance carry over.

Climb the Leagues

Rise from Pathfinder through Vanguard, Warlord and Sovereign to Immortal. Every ranked Season pays out Runes that boost your stats.

Gear up

Fill nine slots from four Gear families. Open chests earned through play, from Common to Legendary, and upgrade Gear with Scrap. No failure chance.

Good to know

  • No ads in this version.
  • Free to play. Reign of Rivals may offer optional in-app purchases of a virtual currency called Crowns, which you can also earn by playing.
  • Tap Continue on the first screen to start playing.
  • Your kingdom is a guest account tied to your device, so note your player name.
  • An internet connection is required.
  • Chat with players in your World, your Alliance and your friends, with translation into 12 languages and built-in mute, block and report tools.
