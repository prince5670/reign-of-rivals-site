Delete your Reign of Rivals account

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Reign of Rivals account (Android or iPhone) and the data linked to it, and what we keep afterwards. You do not need to reinstall the game to ask for deletion.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account. Use one of the options below.

Option 1: Delete in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Reign of Rivals.
  2. Tap Settings (the cog button at the top right of the screen).
  3. Under ACCOUNT, tap MANAGE ACCOUNT.
  4. Under DELETE ACCOUNT, tap ACCOUNT DELETION.
  5. Read the notice, then tap CONFIRM DELETE ACCOUNT.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. The game then goes back to its first screen. If you tap Continue there, the game starts a new, separate kingdom; to stop playing, just close and uninstall the game.

Option 2: Delete on the web (no app needed)

Go to beta.kingdombeta.com/account/delete, our game server's deletion page, and show that the kingdom is yours with one of these:

  • The Google account you linked in the game (Android only: Settings › MANAGE ACCOUNT › LINK GOOGLE). You sign in with Google on that page; we use it only to find the linked kingdom and don't store your email address.
  • A restore code from support, or a transfer code you made in the game that hasn't expired yet. The code is used up by the request and doesn't sign anyone in.

The page shows the kingdom's name and asks you to type DELETE to confirm. The account is then deleted automatically 7 days later. Until then you can change your mind: open the game and tap KEEP MY ACCOUNT in Settings › MANAGE ACCOUNT, or email us.

Option 3: Ask us by email

If you have no linked Google account and no code:

  1. Email reignofrivals@gmail.com with the subject “Reign of Rivals account deletion”.
  2. Tell us your exact player name as it appeared in the game, and roughly when you last played.
  3. If you ever bought Crowns on Android, include a Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) from your Google Play receipt email.

How we check that it's your account

Player names are visible to everyone in the game, so a name alone doesn't prove an account is yours. To stop anyone deleting someone else's kingdom:

  • With a matching Google Play order number, the request counts as proven: the account is deleted 7 days later.
  • With only a name (this includes iPhone players, because App Store receipts can't be matched to an account this way), we still accept the request, but the account is deleted only once it has gone 14 days without being used. If anyone plays the account in the meantime, the request is cancelled automatically, so a stranger can never delete a kingdom that is still being played.
  • We reply to tell you the date. Requests that pass these checks are completed within 30 days.
  • We never send a sign-in code or restore code in reply to a deletion request, and we will never ask for a password (the game doesn't have one).

If the game is still installed on a phone when we delete the account, opening it afterwards starts a new, separate kingdom. Uninstall it if you don't want that.

What gets deleted or removed

  • Your account is closed for good. It can never sign in again. Every sign-in ends and any unused restore or transfer codes stop working.
  • Your name is removed. Your player name becomes “Deleted Player” everywhere it appears, including old chat messages, battle and Scout reports, rankings history, Season results, Alliance notices and city names that used your name. Your old names become free for other players to use. (Our moderation audit records are the one exception; see the table below.)
  • Your Alliance place is released.
  • A linked Google account is unlinked and its ID is deleted.
  • Your push notification tokens are deleted.
  • Your own mute and block lists are deleted.
  • Your chat settings (chat language, Auto-Translate and profanity filter) are deleted, and so are any translations of your messages that were made for other players.
  • Device details (phone model and system version) are removed from any bug or cheating reports you sent.
  • Your activity statistics are deleted, and your player ID is removed from our usage records.
  • On your phone, deleting in the game also removes the stored sign-in.

What happens to your land

  • Your starter area is freed when nothing else depends on it. If the only land you hold is your starting area (your Main City and your five starter cities), and no other player's Marches or safety reports involve it, those cities are normally removed when you delete your account, together with your own Marches to or from them. The space is then reused for a new player.
  • Otherwise your cities stay, without your name. If you captured other cities, or another player's March or safety report involves your starter area, your cities stay on the map, no longer linked to any account, until that Season's World is cleaned up (about 30 days after the Season ends).
  • Land you gave up earlier with Fresh Start has already become public neutral land, and it stays that way.

What we keep, and why

Reign of Rivals is a shared world, so some records stay after you delete your account. They no longer carry your name and are no longer linked to any way of signing in, so nobody, including you, can use or restore them.
